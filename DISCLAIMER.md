## 1. General information only

**Naal Kaalam provides general informational, cultural and educational material. It is not professional advice, an authoritative religious ruling, an official government calendar or a guarantee of future events.**

This applies to Gregorian and Tamil dates, sunrise and sunset, Thithi (திதி), Natchathiram (நட்சத்திரம்), Yogam (யோகம்), Karanam (கரணம்), Nalla Neram (நல்ல நேரம்), Gowri Nalla Neram, Raghu Kaalam (இராகு காலம்), Kuligai (குளிகை), Emagandam (எமகண்டம்), Soolam, remedies, Chandrashtamam, Raasi Palan, festivals, fasting days, public holidays and Subamuhurtham suggestions. It also applies to widgets, reminders and exported calendar cards.

## 2. Calculations and traditions can differ

Panchangam results depend on the calculation method and traditional rules used. Vakya and Drik Ganitha systems, ayanamsa, regional conventions, temple practices, sunrise-based day boundaries, geographic coordinates and time zones can produce different timings or observance dates. Differences need not be only a few minutes.

The app uses precomputed astronomical information for supported reference cities. It does not measure your exact GPS position. Automatic selection from a device time zone may select a reference city some distance from you. Always check the chosen city, time zone and date; daylight-saving changes and travel can affect which civil day a timing belongs to.

Nalla Neram is an app-selected subset of favorable Gowri intervals with specified avoided periods removed. It may not match a printed publisher's Nalla Neram table. Daytime and overnight intervals can also span different civil dates.

We aim to present useful information, but errors, omissions, regional differences and outdated records remain possible. **No accuracy, completeness or universal suitability is guaranteed.**

## 3. Raasi Palan is general reflection

The Raasi Palan feature combines original Tamil reflective guidance with bundled Moon-position and lunar-day information. It does not collect birth details or prepare an individual horoscope. It is not a licensed publisher's daily forecast.

A calculated astronomical position does not guarantee an astrological interpretation or outcome. Do not treat a label such as favorable, caution or Chandrashtamam as proof that a good or bad event will occur. These labels are not diagnoses, risk assessments or instructions to avoid ordinary necessary activities.

## 4. Festivals, religious observances and government holidays

Religious observances can vary by community, denomination, locality and authority. Islamic dates based on tabular calculation are estimates and may change following local moon-sighting announcements. Christian calendar dates can differ among traditions. Verify fasting and temple observances with your own community or institution.

The bundled astronomical calendar presently covers **2026–2030** for supported cities. Tamil Nadu government-holiday entries presently cover **2026 and 2027**, with a documented additional 2026 holiday. This does not establish official government holidays for every later year or the closures of a particular bank, school, employer or court. Official notices may be amended after the app's data was prepared.

For official schedules, consult the latest Tamil Nadu government notice and the institution concerned. Mention of an institution or source does not imply its endorsement of Naal Kaalam.

## 5. Subamuhurtham is an initial date finder

Wedding, housewarming, naming, first-feeding and ear-piercing results are **initial candidates**, based on selected general rules and available daytime intervals. They are not complete personalized muhurtham determinations. Not every birth-chart, compatibility, planetary, seasonal or family-tradition condition is evaluated.

Before arranging a ceremony or paying a deposit, confirm the date, venue, local time and applicable tradition with a qualified priest or practitioner. The app cannot decide whether a particular ceremony date is suitable for a particular person or family.

## 6. No medical, financial, legal or scheduling reliance

Do not use the app as the sole or decisive basis for personal, medical, financial, legal, travel, marriage or event-scheduling decisions. Obtain appropriate professional advice and independently confirm critical information.

In particular, fasting-day information is cultural information, not medical advice to fast or change food, medication or treatment. A qualified healthcare professional should address health questions. Legal deadlines and financial decisions require the appropriate licensed professional or official authority, not an auspicious-time label.

## 7. Reminders, widgets and shared cards

Local alerts and widget refreshes are controlled partly by iOS and can be delayed or missed. The reminder schedule is finite and must be replenished by opening the app. Check its coverage and notification settings, and use a separate reliable reminder for critical commitments.

Morning and fasting reminders follow the selected city's time zone; saved personal alerts follow the device clock. Shared cards are snapshots of the selected day and city and do not receive later corrections. Verify those details before forwarding or relying on a card.
