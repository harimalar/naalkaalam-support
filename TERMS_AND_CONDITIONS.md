Dates and timings can differ across Vakya and Drik Ganitha traditions, ayanamsa choices, regional rules, temple practices, sunrise conventions and methods of assigning an observance to a civil day. Calculations use bundled **reference-city** coordinates and time zones. The app does not measure your GPS location. A city inferred from a time zone is not an exact location match. Confirm the selected city, date and time zone, especially when travelling, comparing India-based observances with an overseas location or crossing a daylight-saving change.

The present bundled calendar covers **2026–2030** for supported reference cities. This is not a promise of complete coverage for every tradition, place or year. Some Hindu observances depend on local religious practice; Islamic festival estimates may change with local crescent announcements; Christian dates can differ by denomination and calendar tradition.

Tamil Nadu government-holiday entries currently include **2026 and 2027** notices and a documented 2026 supplemental holiday. Later official annual lists are not included merely because astronomical calendar data extends to 2030. The app is not a government publication and does not determine school, bank, workplace or court closures. Verify the applicable official notice and institution's schedule.

Subamuhurtham results are **initial candidate dates or windows**, not fully validated, personalized muhurtham recommendations. They do not include every birth-chart, compatibility, seasonal, planetary or family-tradition check. Confirm wedding, housewarming and other ceremony arrangements with the qualified practitioner and venue you intend to use.
