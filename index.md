# Naal Kaalam (நாள்காலம்)

A free, ad-free Tamil calendar for iPhone. Daily Panchangam, general Raasi guidance, festivals, saved dates, reference-city calculations and local reminders are available offline.

## Support

Developed by **Malar Hari (மலர் ஹரி)**.

For help, privacy questions or calendar corrections, email [rajhari@gmail.com](mailto:rajhari@gmail.com). Tamil and English messages are welcome. For a calendar discrepancy, include the selected date and reference city, app version and a source if available. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

The calendar is a cultural and informational reference. Verify important dates, ceremony arrangements and official holidays independently.
