# Naal Kaalam (நாள்காலம்) — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 16, 2026  
**Effective date:** November 15, 2026  
**Developer:** Hari Rajagopalan (creator name: Malar Hari / மலர் ஹரி)  
**Privacy contact:** [rajhari@gmail.com](mailto:rajhari@gmail.com)


## 1. Summary and scope

Naal Kaalam, also styled **NaalKaalam**, is a Tamil daily calendar, Panchangam, Raasi Palan, festival and auspicious-date reference app for iPhone, with companion widgets. It is **100% free, ad-free and offline-first**. The current app has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, account registration or sign-in.

The calendar, bundled city calculations, horoscope guidance, saved dates, widgets and local reminders work without an internet connection. The app does not send personal information, advertising identifiers, usage history or tracking data to the developer or an analytics provider. We do not sell or rent app-user data, share it for advertising, or build advertising profiles.

This policy explains three distinct situations: information processed locally by the app; information you voluntarily send when contacting support or sharing a card; and services operated separately by Apple, website hosts or your chosen sharing provider. “We,” “us” and “our” mean Hari Rajagopalan, the app's developer, who creates apps under the name Malar Hari.

## 2. Information the app does not collect

The app has no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs and no developer-operated backend. It does not automatically collect or upload:

- Your name, email address, telephone number, address or account credentials.
- Contacts, messages, microphone recordings, camera images or the contents of your photo library.
- Precise GPS coordinates, movement history or continuous location information.
- Advertising identifiers, cross-app identifiers, browsing history or app-usage analytics.
- Birth details, medical records, payment information or a personal astrological profile.

There is no remote horoscope API, remote push-notification service, developer cloud account or in-app cloud synchronization. The app does not ask for tracking permission because it does not track you.

## 3. Information stored and processed on your device

The app does store information needed for features you use. This is local processing, not collection by the developer:

| Information | Local purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Selected reference city and its time zone, or the device's time-zone setting | Choose bundled calculations and display dates and times. |
| Appearance, Tamil-number preference and time format | Apply your display choices. |
| Morning and fasting-day reminder preferences | Schedule notifications locally with iOS. |
| Bookmarked calendar dates, event titles, event categories, reference city/time zone and saved-date alert preferences | Maintain your saved-date list and optional reminders. |
| Notification identifiers and scheduling status | Manage pending alerts and show their coverage. |
| A calendar-card image and temporary export file when you choose Share | Prepare the content for the iPhone share sheet. |

Settings needed by the app and its widget are shared through their on-device App Group. This is sharing between components of Naal Kaalam, not disclosure to another company. Saved dates may reveal your interests or plans to someone who can access your device, notifications or backups; the developer cannot read them remotely.

## 4. Location and time-zone choices

City selection is manual, or a reference city is chosen locally from the device's time zone. The app does not request Location Services permission or determine your precise location. Choosing a city does not transmit that choice to us. A reference city's coordinates are bundled calendar inputs, not coordinates measured from your phone.

## 5. Permissions, notifications and widgets

**Notifications are optional.** Permission is requested when you enable a reminder. iOS schedules and delivers these notifications on the device; no server or internet connection is needed. Their visible content can include dates, festival or fasting-day names, timings and saved event titles. You control notification permission and Lock Screen previews in iOS Settings.

Widgets can display calendar information on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Consider who can view those screens. You can remove widgets and turn off reminders without losing access to the calendar.

**Saving an image is optional.** The system may request permission to add an image to Photos if you choose that action. The app does not read or scan your existing photo library.

## 6. Sharing and exported images

A shared calendar card is generated on your device. It can include the selected date, reference city, festivals and timings. Nothing is sent automatically. You select whether to save the card or share it through another app.

After the share sheet finishes or is cancelled, the app attempts to remove its temporary export file. If the app closes unexpectedly, a temporary file may remain until the operating system removes it. Copies you save to Photos, Files or a recipient's service are separate and are not removed by deleting the temporary file or uninstalling Naal Kaalam.

The destination you select may use the internet, synchronize a saved image to the cloud, or process it under its own privacy policy. Review the card and your selected destination before sharing.

## 7. Backups, Apple services and diagnostics

Naal Kaalam does not operate its own backup or synchronization service. Depending on your device settings, local app data can be included in iCloud or computer backups managed by Apple or by you. Saved images may also sync through services you enable. Therefore, “stored locally” does not mean that your device's backup features are disabled. See [Apple's explanation of iCloud backups](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108770).

Apple independently operates the App Store, software updates, device backups and optional system diagnostics. Apple may process information and make platform reports available to developers under its own settings and policies. Naal Kaalam contains no separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK and does not independently upload diagnostic logs. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 8. Support correspondence and public websites

If you email **rajhari@gmail.com**, we receive the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request, investigate a reported issue or meet a legal obligation. It is handled through the email provider; it is not automatically retrieved from the app. Do not send passwords, birth records, medical information or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes and applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to correct or delete it. Access is limited to people who need it to handle the request and service providers involved in delivering or storing the correspondence. We do not use support emails for advertising or sell them. We may disclose correspondence when required by law or necessary to establish or defend a legal claim, subject to applicable protections.

Viewing a hosted legal or support page is different from using the app offline. When these pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. The supplied pages contain no developer-added analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub's handling of website visits is described in its [Pages documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection) and [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). External sites you visit follow their own policies.

## 9. Your choices, retention and deletion

- Change display, city and reminder preferences in the app's settings.
- Remove individual saved dates from the saved-date list. Their associated pending alerts are then removed during reminder reconciliation.
- Disable reminders in the app or revoke notification permission in iOS Settings. Already delivered notifications can be cleared from Notification Center.
- Delete exported copies from the destination where you saved them.
- Use **Delete App**, rather than **Offload App**, to remove the app and its associated local data from the device. Manage any remaining backups or synchronized copies separately through their respective services.

There is no developer-held account or server-side calendar profile to delete. We cannot remotely retrieve, export, correct or erase data that exists only on your device. For information you have sent directly to us, contact the privacy email above to request access, correction or deletion, or exercise other rights available under your local law. We will handle requests as required by applicable law without asking for unnecessary identification.

## 10. Children's privacy

Naal Kaalam is a general-audience cultural reference app and is not directed specifically to children under 13. Its offline features do not ask children to submit personal information or create accounts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the app.

If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent personal information to us, for example by email, please contact **rajhari@gmail.com**. If we learn that we received children's information without authorization required by applicable law, we will delete it or handle it as that law requires. Children should have a parent or guardian contact support for them. This statement describes our practices; it is not a claim of certification under COPPA or any other children's privacy program.

## 11. Security and changes

The app relies on iOS application isolation and the device's security controls. Protect your phone with a passcode and keep iOS updated. No device, backup system or email service can be guaranteed completely secure.

We may update this policy when the app, hosting arrangements or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will show a new effective date. Material changes will receive appropriate notice, and consent will be requested where legally required before introducing new processing. A policy update does not itself enable data collection in an installed offline version.
